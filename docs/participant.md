# Participant Guide

This guide explains how participants use the **Abandoned Institutions Queue System** for Inter-RC Halloween Night 2026.

## Before joining a queue

- One queue ticket covers one group of **1 to 8 people**.
- You can hold an active ticket for more than one haunted house.
- You can hold only one active ticket for the same haunted house in the same browser. If you try again, the website returns you to your existing ticket.
- Choose a team nickname that everyone in your group will remember.
- Do not use real names, phone numbers, email addresses, or other personal information in the nickname.
- Keep this safe word in mind: **Pineapple**. Say it at any time inside a haunted house if you need the experience to stop. Staff will assist you and guide you out.

## 1. Choose a haunted house

1. Open the event website: [irchn-queue.rvrc.workers.dev](https://irchn-queue.rvrc.workers.dev/).
2. Scroll to **Live queues**.
3. Review each haunted house card. It shows:
   - whether the queue is **open**, **paused**, or **closed**;
   - the current estimated wait; and
   - the number of groups waiting.
4. Select a house whose queue is open.

You cannot join a paused or closed queue. If you already have a ticket, it remains viewable when the station changes its queue to paused or closed.

## 2. Join the queue

1. Enter a team nickname between **2 and 30 characters**.
2. Select the number of people in your group, from **1 to 8**.
3. Select **Join _[house name]_ queue**.
4. Wait for the ticket page to appear. Avoid pressing the join button repeatedly while the request is processing.

The website creates one queue number for the entire group, for example `RV-0420`.

## 3. Save and use your ticket

Take a screenshot as soon as the ticket appears. The station master will use the **queue number** and **team nickname** to verify your group at the entrance.

Your ticket displays:

- the haunted house;
- your queue number;
- your team nickname on the browser session used to join;
- the number of groups ahead;
- a live estimated-wait countdown;
- your group size; and
- the ticket status.

Keep the ticket page open or return to it regularly. The website does not send calls, text messages, or push notifications. The ticket checks for new queue information approximately every 30 seconds, or approximately every 15 seconds when five or fewer groups are ahead. Its countdown continues between checks.

When **five or fewer groups** remain ahead of you, make your way towards the haunted-house entrance. The queue may move faster than estimated if other groups do not show up.

Wait estimates differ by haunted house and are only a guide:

| Haunted house | Estimated time per group |
| --- | ---: |
| RVRC | 4 minutes |
| CAPT | 5 minutes |
| Acacia | 7 minutes |
| Tembusu | 10 minutes |
| RC4 | 5 minutes |
| NUSC | 5 minutes |

## Ticket statuses

| Status | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Waiting** | Your group has an active place in line. | Keep checking the ticket and approach the entrance when five or fewer groups remain. |
| **Skipped** | The station could not admit your group when it was called. | Speak to the station master for help. Skipped groups are not included in wait estimates. |
| **Entered** | The station master has admitted your group. | No further queue action is needed. |
| **Cancelled** | The ticket is no longer active. | Speak to event staff if you think this is incorrect. |

## Find an existing ticket

1. Return to the website home page.
2. Scroll to **Find your queue**.
3. Enter the queue number shown in your screenshot.
4. Select **Check status**.

Queue-number lookup is not case-sensitive, and spaces are ignored. A public lookup shows the ticket details but does **not** reveal the team nickname. The nickname is shown only in the browser session that originally joined the queue, so keep the original screenshot for entrance verification.

To join another haunted house, select **Join another queue** on the ticket page and repeat the joining steps.

## If something goes wrong

- **The house cannot be selected:** its queue is paused or closed. Wait for the station to reopen it or choose another house.
- **The ticket cannot be found:** compare the queue number with your screenshot and try again.
- **The page cannot reach the queue:** check your internet connection and retry. Do not create another ticket if your first request may already have succeeded; first use **Find your queue** with the number from your ticket or screenshot.
- **Your ticket says skipped:** speak directly to the station master.
- **The nickname is missing after reopening a ticket:** this is expected for privacy. Use the screenshot taken immediately after joining.

## Privacy reminder

The website stores a random anonymous device token in this browser to prevent duplicate active tickets for the same haunted house. The server stores only a one-way hash of that token; it does not use your IP address or collect a real name, phone number, email address, or NUS ID.
